# 🚀 TEAM EAGLE  
**Blockchain-Powered Green Hydrogen Verification & Carbon Credit Tracking System**

---

## 🌱 Project Overview

To combat climate change, trust and transparency in clean energy data are essential.  
**TEAM EAGLE** is a **blockchain-based system** that tracks green hydrogen production and issues **verifiable digital certificates** that cannot be tampered with or reused — enabling reliable clean energy credits and compliance with global regulations. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

## 🧠 Key Concepts

✔ Green hydrogen certification using blockchain  
✔ Prevents double counting of hydrogen credits  
✔ Verifiable credentials for auditors and regulators  
✔ Ensures electricity used is truly renewable and compliant

## 🔧 Features

### 🌍 Clean Energy Transparency
- Track production of green hydrogen
- Ensure certificates are not double-used
- Align with regulatory frameworks (e.g., RFNBO rules)

### 🔗 Blockchain Integrity
- Immutable distributed ledger
- Smart contracts for automated verification
- Secure, tamper-proof record keeping

### 👥 Verifiable Credentials
- Producers can prove compliance
- Auditors can validate hydrogen origin
- Regulators can enforce clean energy standards
